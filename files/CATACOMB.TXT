CATACOMB: GAME MANUAL
======================

You are standing at the mouth of a dungeon that has been waiting
since 1994. Five levels down. Something old lives at the bottom.

TURNS
-----
You get 20 turns per day. Every action costs one turn: exploring,
descending, resting. Turns reset at midnight, server time. When
you are out of turns, the dungeon closes until tomorrow.

ACTIONS
-------
E: Explore the current level. You may find gold, or something
    with teeth. 6 in 10 explores end in a fight.
D: Descend to the next level. Monsters get stronger the deeper
    you go. The Crypt Lord waits at depth 5.
R: Rest. Recover a third of your maximum health. Safe, but the
    turn is still spent.
S: Show your status again.
Q: Leave the dungeon. Your character is saved exactly as-is.

COMBAT
------
Fights are turn-based. You strike first. Damage is your attack
minus the monster's defense, plus a little luck. If the monster
survives, it strikes back. Defeated monsters drop experience and
gold. Level up and you grow tougher: more health, more attack,
more defense, fully healed.

A: Attack.   F: Flee (free, no shame in it).

DEATH AND VICTORY
-----------------
If your health reaches zero, you die. Your character restarts
from level 1, but the turns you already spent today stay spent.
Deaths are not recorded anywhere.

Defeat the Crypt Lord at depth 5 and your name enters the Hall
of Fame forever, with your final level and the date.

The dungeon is fair: every number it uses is fixed by the rules
above, and the only luck is the die roll in each combat round.
